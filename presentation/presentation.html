Regulatory Landscape

The Patient Consent & Privacy Disconnect

Patients currently lack granular, temporal ownership of their Protected Health Information (PHI).

The Current Flawed Status Quo

❌ Blanket Paper Waivers: One-time signature on admission relinquishes broad data rights indefinitely.
❌ Zero Access Visibility: Patients cannot see which insurers or sub-contractors inspect their history.
❌ No Revocation Mechanism: Once records are shared, patients cannot cancel or expire access.
❌ Centralized Honeypots: Large hospital databases remain prime targets for ransomware attacks.

Regulatory Mandates (DPDP Act 2023 & HIPAA)

✅ Section 6 DPDP Act: Consent must be specific, informed, unconditional, and granular.
✅ Right to Revoke: Consent must be revocable with the same ease with which it was granted.
✅ Data Minimization: Insurers should only access the exact records required for claim verification.
✅ Non-Repudiation: Audit logs must be tamper-proof for court admissibility.

Technical Architecture

The MediLedger Hybrid Architecture

Decoupling clinical storage from cryptographic verification ensures performance, compliance, and scalability.

• Clinical discharge summaries, lab reports, MRI scans remain in off-chain encrypted repositories.
• Preserves patient "Right to be Forgotten" (DPDP Act Section 12).

• Anchors 32-byte cryptographic SHA-256 digests.

Cryptographic Foundation

SHA-256 Fingerprinting & Canonical JSON

Mathematical non-repudiation: guarantees identical files match, while altered files fail instantly.

Deterministic Canonical Serialization

To prevent false-positive hash mismatches caused by key reordering or whitespace:

Hash = SHA-256(CanonicalJSON(EHR))

• Deterministic key sorting (FIPS 180-4 standard)
• Produces consistent 64-character hex string (bytes32)
• Collision probability: ~2-128 (mathematically negligible)

Smart Contract Suite

Modular Solidity Architecture

Four purpose-built contracts deployed on local Hardhat EVM (Solidity 0.8.20).

1. AccessControlManager.sol

Governs RBAC. Registers and verifies accredited Hospitals, Labs, Insurers, and Patients. Blocks unauthorized malicious registrations.

2. RecordRegistry.sol

Maintains mapping of recordId to 32-byte documentHash, issuer, and patient. Provides on-chain verifyRecord(...) method.

3. ConsentManager.sol

Implements access request queue, patient duration approval (1h / 24h / 7d), dynamic expiration checks, and instant revocation.

4. AuditLogger.sol

Append-only chronological event ledger. Permanently records every registration, access attempt, consent grant, and security alert.

Operational Lifecycle

The 5-Step MediLedger Workflow

From initial hospital discharge to automated cashless claim verification.

1. Record Anchoring

Hospital uploads discharge summary off-chain. SHA-256 fingerprint anchored to RecordRegistry.sol.

2. Consent Request

Insurer submits on-chain request citing claim adjudication policy #POL-7731 via ConsentManager.sol.

3. Patient Approval

Patient inspects purpose and grants 48-hour access window directly from their dashboard.

4. Cryptographic Claim Verification

Insurer retrieves off-chain document, hashes it, and verifies it against the smart contract. Authentic records clear in < 4 hours.

5. Instant Revocation & Audit

Patient revokes consent when claim settles. All access and verification events immutably preserved in AuditLogger.sol.

Compliance & Governance

Legal & Regulatory Feasibility

Designed from inception to satisfy Indian and global data privacy legislation.

Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023

• Data Minimization (Sec. 6): Zero plaintext Protected Health Information (PHI) touches the ledger.
• Right to Erasure (Sec. 12): Off-chain record deletion renders on-chain hash an uninvertible string, preserving privacy.
• Granular Revocation (Sec. 6[4]): Patient executes smart contract revocation in 1 click.

IRDAI Health Insurance Standards

• Turnaround Compliance: Accelerates cashless claim settlement to meet IRDAI under-4-hour benchmarks.
• Ombudsman Dispute Non-Repudiation: On-chain immutable audit trail provides conclusive forensic evidence.
• TPA Standardization: Compatible with National Health Claims Exchange (NHCX).

Strategic Defense

Risk Analysis & Mitigation Strategies

Proactive evaluation of technical, operational, and financial risks.

Smart Contract Risk

Risk: Reentrancy or unauthorized state override.

Mitigation: Modular architecture, strictly typed RBAC guards, 100% automated test coverage, and formal security audits.

Hospital Adoption Inertia

Risk: Staff resistance to learning blockchain interfaces.

Mitigation: Background REST API webhooks integrate with existing HIMS software. Clinicians do not change daily routines.

Gas Price Volatility

Risk: Network congestion driving transaction costs up.

Mitigation: Production architecture leverages private consortium EVM or Layer-2 rollups (Polygon CDK / Arbitrum Orbit) for sub-cent gas fees.
